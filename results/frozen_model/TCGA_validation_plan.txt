PRE-SPECIFIED VALIDATION PLAN (written BEFORE any TCGA outcome data were analysed)
Date frozen: 2026-11-10 13:30

MODEL: frozen_signature.rds / signature_coefficients.csv (no re-fitting, no re-selection).
COHORT: TCGA-COAD primary tumours. Exclude rectal (READ) cases, missing OS, OS time <= 0.
PRIMARY ENDPOINT: overall survival (TCGA-CDR columns OS, OS.time). Chosen because the signature
         was trained (GSE39582) and validated (GSE17536) on overall survival.
KEY SECONDARY ENDPOINT: progression-free interval (TCGA-CDR columns PFI, PFI.time).
         Reported regardless of the primary result; it does not replace the primary.
NOT USED: DFI (too few events, excludes stage IV) and DSS (only approximated in TCGA).
DATA SOURCE: TCGA-CDR (Liu et al., Cell 2018), COAD cases only.
SCORING: log2(TPM + 1) from GDC 'STAR - Counts' (assay tpm_unstrand). Z-score each gene across
         the eligible TCGA-COAD primary tumours (one sample per patient, valid OS);
         score = sum(coefficient x z). Genes matched by HGNC symbol, then by Ensembl ID via
         org.Hs.eg.db aliases. Missing genes are dropped with coefficients unchanged.
         If fewer than 90% of the signature genes are present, STOP and discuss.
COVARIATES: stage collapsed to I-II / III / IV (TCGA-CDR ajcc_pathologic_tumor_stage), age, sex.

PRIMARY ANALYSIS: Cox model, hazard ratio per 1 SD of the TCGA score,
         adjusted for stage (I-II / III / IV), age at diagnosis, sex.
PRIMARY SUCCESS CRITERION (overall survival, adjusted HR per 1 SD of the score):
  SUPPORTED    : HR > 1 and the 95% CI excludes 1.
  INCONCLUSIVE : HR > 1 but the 95% CI includes 1 (low power is expected; NOT called a failure).
  NOT SUPPORTED: HR <= 1.
  No other threshold, endpoint or subgroup may be substituted after seeing results.

SECONDARY: same adjusted model with PFI as the endpoint; high vs low by TCGA-cohort median;
         added value over stage+age+sex
         (likelihood-ratio test, C-index); MSI/MMR-adjusted model if MSI status is available.
SENSITIVITY: follow-up capped at 60 months; excluding stage IV.

NOT ALLOWED after seeing TCGA outcomes: changing genes, coefficients, cutoffs, covariates,
or the success criterion. Any deviation is reported as post-hoc.
